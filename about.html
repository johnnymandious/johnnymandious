ABOUT ME.

Background, thoughts on AI, and the intent behind this site.

Welcome to my website.

I have a lot to say about AI, I'm learning as I go and I'm interested in understanding models, what they do, where they fail and where the future of AI will lead us and what it will take to get there.

I don't think AI is necessarily bad, I think it will drastically change the world through healthcare, economically and socially but I do think restrictions will be needed as it's capabilities increases.

This website is made by AI but all my writing and things I produce outside my website will always be made by me.
